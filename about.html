About STS7

Professional technology solutions focused on practical business and individual needs.

Who We Are

Salv-7 Tech Solutions is a newly established technology services company founded in 2026 in Tzaneen, Limpopo. The business was created to provide affordable and reliable technology services to individuals, small businesses, start-ups and local organisations.

Our services include website development, software development, IT support, computer repairs and networking.

Our Focus

  • Practical technology solutions
  • Clear and accessible communication
  • Reliable support
  • Solutions suited to client needs

Our Mission and Vision

STS7 is built around affordable, reliable and useful technology.

Mission

To provide individuals and small organisations with affordable, reliable and quality technology solutions that help them improve their work, overcome technical challenges and achieve their goals.

Vision

To grow from a trusted local technology provider into a recognised technology company serving larger organisations through innovative and reliable technology solutions.
