## Project Overview

STS7 is a newly established technology services company founded in 2026 in Tzaneen, Limpopo. The company was created in response to the growing need for affordable and reliable technology services among local individuals and small businesses. STS7 aims to provide practical technology solutions, including website development, software development, computer repairs, networking and IT support. As a new business, STS7 intends to build a strong reputation through quality services, customer satisfaction and continued growth within the local community.

## Website Goals & Objectives

### Goals

• Inform visitors about STS7 — clearly explain who STS7 is, its mission, vision and what the company does.
• Showcase STS7's services — present the technology services offered, including website development, software development, computer repairs, networking, IT support and consulting.
• Generate potential clients — encourage visitors who need technology services to consider STS7.
• Allow customers to request quotations — provide an easy way for potential clients to describe their needs and request a quotation.
• Showcase previous work/projects — demonstrate STS7's capabilities through a portfolio of completed or sample projects.
• Make it easy to contact STS7 — provide clear contact information and an enquiry/contact form for visitors.

### KPIs

• KPI 1: Attract 50–70 website visitors per month during the first few months. Increase website traffic by 30% within three months.
• KPI 2: Have at least 40% of visitors view the Services page.
• KPI 3: At least 10 potential client enquiries per month.
• KPI 4: At least 5 quotation requests per month.
• KPI 5: At least 30% of visitors view the Portfolio/Projects page.
• KPI 6: Receive at least 10 contact/enquiry submissions per month.
