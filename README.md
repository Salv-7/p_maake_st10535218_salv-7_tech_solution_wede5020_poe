## Project Overview

STS7 is a newly established technology services company founded in 2026 in Tzaneen, Limpopo. The company was created in response to the growing need for affordable and reliable technology services among local individuals and small businesses.

STS7 aims to provide practical technology solutions, including website development, software development, computer repairs, networking, IT support and technology consulting. As a new business, STS7 intends to build a strong reputation through quality services, customer satisfaction and continued growth within the local community.

The website was developed as part of the WEDE5020 Web Development (Introduction) Portfolio of Evidence (PoE). The website introduces the company, presents its services, showcases sample projects and provides visitors with ways to make enquiries or request quotations.

---

# Website Goals and Objectives

## Goals

- Inform visitors about STS7 by clearly explaining who the company is, its mission, vision and services.
- Showcase STS7's technology services.
- Generate potential client enquiries.
- Allow potential customers to request quotations.
- Showcase previous and sample projects through a portfolio.
- Make it easy for visitors to contact STS7.
- Provide a consistent and accessible user experience across different devices.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- **KPI 1:** Attract 50–70 website visitors per month during the first few months and increase website traffic by 30% within three months.
- **KPI 2:** Have at least 40% of visitors view the Services page.
- **KPI 3:** Receive at least 10 potential client enquiries per month.
- **KPI 4:** Receive at least 5 quotation requests per month.
- **KPI 5:** Have at least 30% of visitors view the Previous Projects page.
- **KPI 6:** Receive at least 10 contact or enquiry submissions per month.

These KPIs are proposed targets for the future performance of the website.
